# Changelog 2.0.1

## 🚀 Dual-Platform Engine: Paper & Fabric First-Class Support

- **Multi-Module Gradle Architecture**:
  - Restructured the entire codebase into a clean, modern multi-module architecture:
    - `:api`: Platform-independent public interfaces, models, and events (`DreamVoiceAPI`, `VoiceLocation`, `Speaker`, `VoiceWiretap`, `VoiceEvent`, etc.).
    - `:common`: Platform-agnostic core acoustic engine (`DreamVoiceCommon`), DSP audio pipelines, raycasting logic, persistence, and service implementations.
    - `:paper`: Paper / Purpur 26.1.2+ server plugin (`DreamVoice-paper-2.0.1.jar`) utilizing Paperweight and shadow jar packaging.
    - `:fabric`: Fabric 26.1.2+ server mod (`DreamVoice-fabric-2.0.1.jar`) built with Fabric Loom 1.17, Fabric API, and Cloud Fabric commands.
- **Platform Abstraction Layer (`VoicePlatform`)**:
  - Implemented `PaperVoicePlatform` and `FabricVoicePlatform` bridging server scheduling (sync/async/timer), entity tracking, block occlusion raycasts, particle dispatches, and loggers.
  - Native multiplatform coordinates via `VoiceLocation`, allowing services to operate identically regardless of whether Bukkit or Minecraft ServerLevel is running underneath.
- **Dual Configuration Directories**:
  - Automatically loads and saves data in standard platform locations:
    - **Paper**: `plugins/DreamVoice/`
    - **Fabric**: `config/dreamvoice/`

## 🔌 Unified `DreamVoiceAPI` Service Locator

- **Universal API Entrypoint**:
  - Unified entry point `DreamVoiceAPI.get()` accessible from both Paper plugins and Fabric mods without platform-specific boilerplate.
  - Direct accessor methods for all acoustic services:
    - `api.speakerService()`: 3D spatial speakers, dual-channel audio, entity tracking.
    - `api.wallService()`: VoiceWall raycast occlusion, material decibel loss, diffraction.
    - `api.roomService()`: Soundproof Cuboid zones and reverberation presets.
    - `api.recordingService()`: Zero-CPU Opus capture, timeline slicing, interactive cassettes.
    - `api.radioService()`: Frequency channels, analog band-pass filters, push-to-talk clicks.
    - `api.wiretapService()`: Covert listening bugs, mobile entity bugs, live eavesdropping.
    - `api.projectionService()`: Decoupled voice anchors, CCTV feeds, bilateral audio.
    - `api.transmitterService()`: Point-to-point directional voice transmitters.
    - `api.broadcastService()`: Server-wide and localized podium/intercom broadcasts.
    - `api.filterService()`: File-first DSP filters, hot-compilation, and stack management.
    - `api.speechService()`: Offline Vosk keyword spotting and transcription stations.
    - `api.playerService()`: Voice player wrappers (`VPlayer`) and connection metadata.
    - `api.persistenceService()`: Asynchronous disk flushes and auto-save coordination.
    - `api.voiceService()`: Direct Simple Voice Chat bridge and raw packet dispatch.

## 👁️ Real-Time 3D Acoustic Visualizer Mode (`/voice visualizer`)

- **Interactive Acoustic Raycasting**:
  - Added `/voice visualizer [toggle|on|off]` (permission: `dreamvoice.visualizer`).
  - Renders 3D particle rays illustrating line-of-sight paths, wall collision impacts, aperture diffraction bypasses around doors/openings, and speaker audible coverage spheres.
- **Performance-Conscious Hand-Held Requirement**:
  - Particle streams are rendered **only** when the inspecting player has the visualizer tool (default: `COMPASS`) equipped in their main hand, preventing client lag and visual clutter during normal gameplay.
- **Synchronized Visualizer State**:
  - State is automatically synced to Fabric clients via `ServerBoundVisualizerTogglePacket` and `ClientBoundVisualizerSyncPacket`.

## 🎙️ VoiceWiretap x VoiceProjection Integration

- **Cross-Module Audio Propagation**:
  - Covert wiretap microphones (`VoiceWiretap`) now dynamically support and intercept voices emitted through active voice projections (`VoiceProjection`).
- **Dual-Origin Spatial Audio**:
  - **Physical Emission (`isEmitVoiceAtPlayer`)**: Microphones in proximity to the player's physical body capture the direct voice as normal.
  - **Anchor Emission (`isEmitVoiceAtAnchor`)**: Microphones in proximity to the projection's anchor location (`projection.getAnchorLocation()`) intercept the projected voice in real time.
  - **Acoustic Occlusion & Wall Physics**: Line-of-sight raycasting (`VoiceRayCast`) and material attenuation (`VoiceWall`) are accurately simulated between the anchor coordinates and the wiretap.
  - **Filter Inheritance**: Voice projections configured with custom filters (e.g., megaphone, radio, disguise) pass their processed audio downstream to wiretap listeners.

## 🔌 Public Hearing & Attenuation API (`VoiceService` & `VoiceWallService`)

- **Unified Audibility Check (`canHear`)**:
  - Introduced `voiceService.canHear(speaker, listener)` (with `Player` and `UUID` overloads).
  - Evaluates whether a listener can hear a speaker across all communication channels:
    1. Direct point-to-point audio transmitters (`VoiceTransmitterService`).
    2. Shared radio frequency channels (`VoiceRadioService`).
    3. Proximity spatial voice chat (`canHearProximity`).
- **Full-Condition Proximity Audibility (`canHearProximity`)**:
  - Introduced `voiceService.canHearProximity(speaker, listener)` (with `Player` and `UUID` overloads).
  - Validates active Simple Voice Chat connections for both players.
  - Enforces life states (`ALIVE`, `DEAD`, `SPECTATE`).
  - Evaluates voice projection constraints (`emitVoiceAtPlayer`, `emitVoiceAtAnchor`, `hearPlayerEnvironment`).
  - Respects configured distance limits (`Codex.getEffectiveDistance()`).
  - Computes total dB attenuation across `VoiceWall` materials and isolated `VoiceRoom` cuboids; returns `false` if sound is blocked ($\ge 99.0\text{ dB}$).
- **Direct Decibel Attenuation Metrics**:
  - Added `voiceService.getEffectiveAttenuationDb(speaker, listener)` to measure total acoustic dB loss between two players.
  - Added `voiceWallService.getAttenuationDb(speaker, listener)` for querying raw wall and room isolation losses.

## 📢 Physical Broadcast System (`VoiceBroadcastService`)

- **Podium & Intercom Broadcast Points**:
  - Introduced `VoiceBroadcastService` and `/voice broadcast` command suite.
  - Players speaking within a configured capture radius around a location or entity automatically have their voice routed to connected speakers.
- **Flexible Audio Routing**:
  - **All Speakers Routing (`allSpeakers = true`)**: Broadcasts to every registered speaker across the server.
  - **Selective Target Speakers**: Link specific speakers to a broadcast point (`/voice broadcast link <name> <speaker>`).
- **On/Off Toggle & Filter Chaining**:
  - Broadcast points can be toggled on/off on demand (`/voice broadcast toggle <name>`).
  - Optional custom audio filters can be chained to the broadcast (e.g. intercom distortion, PA megaphone filter).
- **Modular Persistence**:
  - Stored under `modules/broadcast/` with automatic saving on server shutdown.

  - **Acoustic Rooms Inspector (`RoomListGUI`)**: View room dimensions, reverberation presets, and acoustic isolation percentages.
  - **Radio Channels Manager (`RadioListGUI`)**: Browse frequencies, view active listener counts, and tune in/disconnect directly.
  - **Transmitters Manager (`TransmitterListGUI`)**: Toggle transmitter mode, view linked receiver players, unlink or configure receivers.
  - **Projections Inspector (`ProjectionListGUI`)**: Inspect active player projections, emission modes (anchor/player), and teleport to anchor coordinates.
  - **Acoustic & Wall Engine (`AcousticInspectGUI`)**: Live toggle for VoiceWall, cycle occlusion modes (Realistic/Strict/Off), toggle air damping, particle raycast debug, and inspect acoustic properties of targeted blocks/materials.

## 🐛 Bug Fixes & Stability

- **Projection Audio Crackling & Transcoding Artifacts**:
  - Eliminated audio crackling and high-frequency buzzing occurring when speaking while in voice projection:
    - Removed discontinuous chunk-level 1-pole lowpass smoothing filter that injected 50 Hz boundary discontinuities into audio packets.
    - Prevented multi-decoding of incoming microphone Opus packets on shared decoders across listeners.
    - Implemented `encoder.resetState()` on speech pauses (> 400ms) to prevent Opus predictor state corruption upon resuming speech.
    - Bypassed PCM decoding and lossy Opus transcoding completely when no wall occlusion or voice filters are present, routing raw Opus packets directly to 3D locational audio channels.
- **Speech Recognizer Crash on Reload**:
  - Resolved `kaldi::KaldiFatalError` (`Can't add speaker model to already running recognizer`) triggered during `/voice speech reload` by safely closing and cycling active Vosk recognizer instances before applying updated keyword grammars.
