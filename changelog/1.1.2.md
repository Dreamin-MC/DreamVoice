# Changelog 1.1.2

## ✨ New Features

- **Audio File Export (MP3, OGG, WAV)**:
  - Added asynchronous audio export to `VoiceRecordingService`: `exportRecording(UUID/VoiceRecording, AudioExportFormat, [fileName])`.
  - Converts audio into MP3, OGG Vorbis, or standard PCM WAV into `plugins/DreamVoice/exports/`.
  - Decodes Opus speech stream directly and accurately using sequential stream reconstruction with natural conversational pause detection, eliminating distortion and crackling.
  - Added command: `/record export <id> <mp3|ogg|wav> [fileName]` with tab-completion, custom destination file naming, and direct file path feedback.

## 🐛 Bug Fixes & Audio Improvements

- **Fixed VoiceWallOcclusionEvent Async Dispatch Exception**:
  - Resolved `java.lang.IllegalStateException: VoiceWallOcclusionEvent may only be triggered synchronously` occurring when `VoiceWallServiceImpl.processEntitySoundPacket` dispatched acoustic occlusion checks on Simple Voice Chat's audio processing thread (`Server$ProcessThread`).
  - `VoiceWallOcclusionEvent` now explicitly calls `super(true)` in its constructor to mark the Bukkit event as asynchronous, allowing Paper's `PaperEventManager` to dispatch it safely across worker threads without exceptions.
  - Hardened `EntitySoundPacketEvent` with an explicit asynchronous constructor (`super(true)`) to prevent similar thread check violations.

- **Restored Speaker Audio Feedback for Nearby Senders**:
  - Fixed an issue where players speaking into or next to a 3D speaker could not hear their own voice coming out of the speaker.
  - Removed sending-player exclusions (`!sp.getUuid().equals(senderUuid)` in `broadcastToDedicatedChannels` and `if (listener.getUniqueId().equals(senderUuid)) return;` in `processSingleListenerStream`).
  - Players standing within the speaker's acoustic range (`distance`) and line-of-sight now naturally hear their voice projected from the speaker's 3D locational coordinates.
  - Integrated `speaker.getFilter()` listener validation within `processSingleListenerStream` to ensure custom speaker recipient predicates are consistently respected across both direct streaming and DSP-filtered pipelines.

- **Complete DSP Voice Filter Overhaul**:
  - **Radio (`RadioVoiceFilter`)**: Replaced 10-bit stair-step quantization (which caused harsh clicking/crackling) and 1st-order IIR with a cascaded 4th-order Butterworth bandpass (480 Hz – 2600 Hz, 24 dB/oct), mid-range presence boost (+4 dB at 1.5 kHz), warm hyperbolic tangent ($\tanh$) analog overdrive, and subtle RF carrier hiss (-42 dB).
  - **Telephone (`TelephoneVoiceFilter`)**: Implemented 2nd-order Butterworth bandpass (350 Hz – 3400 Hz) with vintage carbon capsule asymmetrical saturation.
  - **Alien (`AlienVoiceFilter`)**: Implemented linear fractional-delay interpolation on the sinus modulation line, eliminating sample-skipping clicks.
  - **Ghost (`GhostVoiceFilter`)**: Added smooth linear delay interpolation on dual chorusing delay lines with soft-knee limiting to eliminate crackling.
  - **Helium & Deep (`HeliumVoiceFilter`, `DeepVoiceFilter`)**: Completely redesigned pitch-shifting using 40ms grains with Hann windowing (ensuring constant unity overlap-add gain) and linear interpolation, eliminating the 50 Hz buzz and window boundary clicks.
  - **Cave (`CaveVoiceFilter`)**: Rebalanced wet/dry mix (0.68/0.32) and added soft limiting to prevent reverb buffer overflow and distortion on loud speech.
  - **Megaphone (`MegaphoneVoiceFilter`)**: 2nd-order horn bandpass (600 Hz – 3400 Hz) with +5 dB acoustic throat resonance at 1100 Hz, analog saturation, and normalized slapback echo.
  - **Robot (`RobotVoiceFilter`)**: Normalized carrier synthesis to strictly $\le 1.0$ and tuned ring modulation dry/wet balance.
  - **Disguise (`DisguiseVoiceFilter`)**: Fixed buffer index clamping bug, isolated states per player UUID to prevent cross-player modulation bleeding, and added linear interpolation.
