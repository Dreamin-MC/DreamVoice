# Changelog 2.0.2

## 🎙️ Multi-Speaker Tracking & Selective Voice Recording

- **Per-Frame Speaker Attribution**:
  - `TimedAudioFrame` now tags every audio frame with the speaker's unique identifier (`speakerUuid`).
- **Multi-Speaker Track Management in `VoiceRecording`**:
  - `getSpeakers()`: Returns all distinct players who spoke during the recording session.
  - `getFramesOfSpeaker(UUID)`: Retrieves audio frames filtered by speaker.
  - `extractSpeakerRecording(UUID)`: Isolates and generates an independent recording containing only the selected speaker's voice.
- **Enhanced `.dv` Audio Persistence**:
  - Serializes and deserializes speaker UUIDs per frame while preserving backward compatibility with older format revisions.
- **Selective Audio Export**:
  - `VoiceRecordingService.exportRecording(rec, format, targetFile, speakerUuid)`: Allows exporting either full mixed audio or an isolated individual speaker track to WAV, MP3, or OGG.
  - `VoiceRecordingService.extractSpeakerRecording(recUuid, speakerUuid)`: Direct service extraction helper.
- **Wiretap Speaker Tagging**:
  - Intercepted audio streams captured by `VoiceWiretapService` now track speaker UUIDs on every recorded frame.

---

## 📻 Radio Channel Recording & Broadcasting

- **Full Radio Channel Capture**:
  - Added radio recording APIs: `startRecording(channel)`, `stopRecording(channel)`, `isRecording(channel)`, and `getActiveRecording(channel)`.
  - Captures all conversations transmitted across a radio frequency with complete speaker attribution.
- **Radio Playback Streaming**:
  - Added radio playback APIs: `playRecordingToRadio(channel, recording)`, `stopRadioPlayback(channel)`, and `isRadioPlaying(channel)`.
  - Seamlessly broadcasts pre-recorded audio or intercepted transmissions back over radio channels.
- **Zero-CPU Direct Opus Streaming (`OpusAudioPlayer`)**:
  - Lightweight audio player streams raw Opus packets directly into Simple Voice Chat static audio channels without decoding and re-encoding overhead.
  - Supports dynamic `frameConsumer` callbacks per frame for real-time synchronization with external addons.

---

## 📡 Radio Transmission Filtering & Packet Interception (`RadioPacketFilter`)

- **Bidirectional Packet Filter Interface (`RadioPacketFilter`)**:
  - `shouldTransmit(channel, senderUuid)`: Intercepts transmissions at the sender microphone stage before recording or dispatching. Enables mods (such as AcoustiCraft) to block transmissions if players are out of antenna range or lacking power/equipment.
  - `shouldDeliver(channel, senderUuid, receiverUuid)`: Intercepts delivery at the receiver stage to simulate range attenuation, line-of-sight wall penetration, and distance falloff.
  - Registered and accessed via `VoiceRadioService.setPacketFilter(...)` and `VoiceRadioService.getPacketFilter()`.
- **New `RadioTransmissionEvent`**:
  - Dispatched whenever voice audio is transmitted over a radio frequency, providing access to the channel, sender UUID, and raw Opus audio data.
- **Audio Transmission Listeners**:
  - Added `RadioAudioTransmissionListener` and `VoiceRadioService.addAudioTransmissionListener(...)` to subscribe to broadcast frames.
- **Cleaner Voice Output**:
  - Removed artificial RF hiss generator from `RadioVoiceFilter` for cleaner and more intelligible voice communications.

---

## ⌨️ New Commands (Fabric & Paper)

- **Radio Recording Management**:
  - `/record radio start <channel>`: Starts recording audio from a specific radio channel.
  - `/record radio stop <channel> [target]`: Stops recording and saves the captured radio stream.
  - `/record radio play <channel> <recording>`: Plays back an audio recording over a radio channel.
  - `/record radio stop-play <channel>`: Halts an active radio broadcast.
- **Audio Timeline Slicing**:
  - `/record slice <recording> <startMs> <endMs> [target]`: Cuts out a precise timestamp window from an existing recording.
  - `/record slice-last <recording> <durationSec> [target]`: Quickly slices the last N seconds of an active or saved recording.
- **Speaker-Filtered Export**:
  - `/record export <id> <format> [speaker]`: Exports an audio recording to disk with optional player filtering.
